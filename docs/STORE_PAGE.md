# Store page text (Modrinth / CurseForge)

## Summary (one line)

Crew-based pirate sailing in the spirit of Sea of Thieves: a real, walkable Sloop, Brigantine and Galleon with
sails, cannons, holes, sinking, a brig, crews and captains.

## Suggested tags

Adventure, Transportation, Multiplayer, Game Mechanics. Environment: client and server (required on both).
Dependencies: Sable (required). Optional: Patchouli, Jade, JEI, EMI, JourneyMap, Open Parties and Claims,
FTB Chunks, FTB Teams, Simple Voice Chat.

## Description

**Set sail.** Seafarers puts a real ship under your feet: built of blocks, floating on the sea, and yours to
walk while she moves. Steer her, trim her sails to the wind, fire her guns, patch her hull, bail her hold and,
if it comes to it, go down with her.

All three ships are here, fully fitted out: **the Sloop**, **the Brigantine** and **the Galleon**, each with her
own handling, crew size and decks, and a **brig** on the two bigger ships for crewmates who've earned it.

### Your ship
- Choose your ship the first time you join, and launch her from her deed on open water. You're her captain.
- Sleep in her bunk and you'll respawn aboard her wherever she's sailed.
- A Ship's Compass always points the way back to her.

### Sailing
- A heavy, smooth wheel, sails you work by hand at their ropes, a capstan to heave, and a wind that shifts.
- A blank sea chart on the map table, as in Sea of Thieves: drag it, zoom it, and mark your X.
- Drop the anchor at speed with the wheel hard over for a tight anchor turn.
- Her status at a glance while you're aboard: speed, the wind, every sail, water and holes.

### Fighting
- Cannons with cannonballs, chain shot, firebombs and eight cursed cannonballs, with a camera that follows the gun.
- Harpoon guns to grab loot or pull yourself to shore or to another ship.
- Climb into a cannon and fire yourself across the sea.

### Keeping her afloat
- Holes let the sea in, running aground holes her, fire spreads, masts break. Patch, bail and mend, or she goes down, and a new ship waits
  for her crew nearby. Her stores float up in shipwreck barrels for whoever gets there first.
- Fell overboard and got left behind? A mermaid comes to take you back aboard. Run aground? Have her towed off.

### With friends
- Invite crew at the quest table, lock your ship so only your crew can use her, hand her to another captain or
  swap her for a new one.
- Rowboats wait along the coasts for anyone to take.

### For servers
- Admin commands to list, find, lock, hand over or remove any ship, and permission nodes for LuckPerms.
- A server-wide ship limit, a wait between free ships, abandoned ships that sink on their own, and locked ships
  that shrug off creepers and TNT.

### Built to fit in
- No mixins of its own; vanilla is left alone. Storage works with hoppers and storage mods, the stove cooks any
  mod's food, and map mods open from the map table.
- About 60 settings, each explained in plain English, for single player, friends' servers or pirate servers.
- Hints the first time you use each thing aboard, and 20 advancements to work through.

### Works with (all optional)
- **Patchouli:** the Captain's Handbook, an in-game guide to everything.
- **Jade:** look at a ship to see her captain, crew, water and lock.
- **JEI / EMI:** info pages for the mod's items.
- **JourneyMap:** a waypoint that follows your ship. Map mods also open from the map table.
- **Open Parties and Claims, FTB Chunks / FTB Teams:** no launching in others' claims; your party or team can use
  your locked ship.
- **Simple Voice Chat:** a voice group for each crew.

Fan-made. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rare or Microsoft. No Sea of Thieves assets are used.
